# Field interview questions

Eight questions each for traders and forwarders. Ask open questions, listen for real
numbers and real stories, and don't pitch until the end. Ask permission before recording,
and note the date, location and role (no full names unless they agree).

## Traders (importers)

1. **How do you pay your supplier today?** Walk me through your last order: who you paid,
   in what currency (naira, USDT, RMB), through whom, and when.
2. **What did that cost you?** Agent or payment-agent fees, FX spread, transfer fees, in
   naira or as a percentage.
3. **Has an order ever gone wrong?** Wrong goods, short quantity, damaged or missing cartons,
   or an agent who disappeared. What happened, how much did you lose, and what did you do?
4. **How do you choose a forwarder or consolidator?** Recommendations, WhatsApp groups,
   price, past experience? What would make you switch?
5. **How long is your money tied up?** From paying the supplier to having goods in your
   shop, in weeks. How do you finance that time?
6. **What proof do you get before goods ship?** Photos, videos, packing lists? Do you trust
   them?
7. **Do you use USDT or other stablecoins today?** For what, through which app or P2P
   channel? Would you keep money in a dollar stablecoin escrow for 45–60 days?
8. **If your payment stayed locked until you approved photos of your cartons, would you pay
   0.75% of the goods value for that?** What would make you say no?

## Forwarders (consolidators)

1. **How many containers do you run a month, on which routes,** and how many traders share
   one container?
2. **How do traders pay you for freight?** When, in what currency, and how often do you
   chase late payments?
3. **How do new traders decide to trust you?** What do they ask for? How long does it take
   to win a new trader?
4. **What goes wrong most often?** Disputes over volume (CBM), missing cartons, damaged
   goods, traders who never collect. Who pays when it happens?
5. **What do you photograph or record at the China warehouse today?** How do you share it
   with traders?
6. **Would you post a guarantee** (a deposit traders can see, which pays them if cartons go
   missing after loading) if it brought you more bookings? How big could it be?
7. **Would a public track record help you?** Deliveries, on-time arrivals, disputes. What
   would you want it to show, and what would worry you?
8. **If freight were paid into escrow at booking and released to you at pickup, would you
   run a container through Manifest?** What would you need first?

## After each interview

Write down: the role, the biggest pain in their words, any numbers (fees, losses, volumes,
days), whether they'd try a pilot, and whether they'd sign `docs/LOI_TEMPLATE.md`.
