_Template for a forwarder or trader who wants to pilot Manifest. Fill in the brackets,
print or send as PDF, and ask for a signature or a written "I agree" by message. Keep a
copy; with the signer's permission, quote it in the hackathon submission._

**Date:** [date]

**From:** [Full name], [Company / shop name], [Role: freight forwarder / importer]
[Address or market location, e.g. Computer Village, Ikeja] · [Phone / WhatsApp] · [Email]

**To:** Emediong Etuk Gregory, founder of Manifest

**Subject:** Letter of intent to pilot Manifest

I have reviewed Manifest, a pay-on-proof escrow service for goods shipped from China to
Nigeria in shared containers, in which:

- the trader's payment is locked in escrow when they book space in a container;
- the forwarder photographs and measures the goods at the China warehouse;
- the supplier is paid only after the trader approves the photos;
- the forwarder posts a guarantee that can compensate traders for lost goods; and
- freight is released to the forwarder when the trader collects the goods in Lagos.

**I intend to pilot Manifest** as follows (tick one):

- [ ] **Forwarder:** run [number] container(s) on the [origin] → [destination] route
      through Manifest between [month] and [month] [year], with about [number] traders per
      container, and post a guarantee of about [amount] USD.
- [ ] **Trader:** book [number] shipment(s) of about [amount] USD of goods through Manifest
      between [month] and [month] [year].

What matters most to me: [e.g. getting paid for freight faster / not losing money to
agents / proving my reliability to new traders].

This letter is **not binding**. It does not create any legal obligation, payment
commitment or exclusivity for either party. It records my genuine interest in testing the
service, subject to Manifest being available on mainnet and to terms we agree later.

I agree that Manifest may mention [my name and company / my company name only / this
letter anonymously] in its hackathon submission and investor materials.
