1/ Small importers in Lagos send money to agents in China and pray. Sixty days later they

I built Manifest: pay-on-proof escrow for traders who ship in shared containers. Live on
Solana devnet. 🧵

2/ How it works:
→ Book space in a forwarder's container; your money locks in a Solana escrow
→ The forwarder photographs and measures your cartons at the China warehouse
→ You approve, and only then is your supplier paid
→ You get a Cargo Ticket for the goods at sea

3/ The Cargo Ticket is a Token-2022 NFT: it carries the right to collect the goods and to
open a dispute. Sell it, and the buyer collects in Lagos.

Every forwarder posts a guarantee. Disputes go to a Squads multisig that can pay
you from it.
