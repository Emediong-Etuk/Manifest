# Manifest for freight forwarders

**Fill your containers faster. Get paid for freight without chasing anyone.**

## The problem you know

Traders hesitate to send money to a forwarder they don't know. Good forwarders lose
bookings to cheap ones because nobody can tell them apart. At pickup you chase traders for
the freight balance.

## How Manifest works for you

1. **Open a container** on Manifest: route, space, rate per CBM, cut-off and arrival date.
   You get a link to share on WhatsApp (it shows a preview card with space left and your
   rate).
2. **Traders book and pay into escrow.** Their goods payment and the freight are locked in
   Manifest the moment they book. You can see every booking.
3. **At your China warehouse**, photograph and measure each trader's cartons in the app.
   The trader approves on their phone and the supplier is paid automatically.
4. **Load, sail, arrive.** Mark the container loaded and arrived in two taps.
5. **At pickup in Lagos**, scan the trader's pickup code. **The freight is released to you
   instantly**, from escrow, no chasing.

## Why traders will choose you

- **Your guarantee:** you lock a deposit (the guarantee) in Manifest. Traders see it on your
  page. It protects them if cartons go missing after loading, so they trust you sooner.
- **Your public record:** every delivery, on-time arrival and dispute builds your Manifest
  Score. Nobody can fake it, so your good work finally counts.
- **Freight is safe:** freight is paid into escrow at booking, measured at your warehouse,
  and paid to you at pickup. If a trader never comes, you claim it yourself after the pickup
  period.

## What it costs you

Nothing per container during the pilot. Manifest charges traders a 0.75% fee on the goods
value. Your guarantee stays yours: you can withdraw what isn't backing open bookings.
