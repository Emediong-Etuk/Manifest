# Pitch script (≤ 3 minutes)

Colosseum's guidance: no more than three minutes; cover the team's background, the problem,
who it's for, any user feedback or validation, and the broader vision
([source](https://blog.colosseum.com/perfecting-your-hackathon-submission/)). Confirm the
current limit in the Arena form before recording. About 400 words at a calm pace.

Record the product section (0:50–1:50) as a phone screen recording on devnet; the rest can
be Greg on camera with a few full-screen slides (numbers, the flow diagram, the roadmap).

## Notes

- Fill the bracketed validation line with real numbers from `docs/INTERVIEW_QUESTIONS.md`
  interviews and signed `docs/LOI_TEMPLATE.md` letters. Leave it out rather than estimate.
- Numbers and sources are in the README's "The problem" section. The $750 per container is
  an illustration (25 shipments × $4,000 average), say "around".
- Keep the product section real: no mockups. Seeded data is fine; say it's devnet.
- Two lines depend on setup that isn't done yet: "sign in with Google" needs a Phantom
  Portal App ID (otherwise say "connect Phantom"), and "live on devnet today" needs the
  devnet deploy. Change them if either is still missing when you record.
